# Final unrestricted four-search experiment report

## Protocol and counting

Search execution used 4,187 representatives after alpha-renaming and
equality-side-symmetry deduplication.  The two test sets are reported
separately: 130 order-five records and 4,141 ALPS records (4,140 equivalence
classes).  There are 83 cross-source equivalence classes and one redundant
record within ALPS, so the two source denominators must not be added.  Solver
generation was performed only in remote Sandboxes.  Every
profile had an independent 120-second internal search budget per problem, a
125-second outer allowance, a 2,000 MiB process-tree memory ceiling, and a
1,024,000-byte certificate ceiling.  The stable generation pool used 120
concurrent Sandboxes, below the authorized cap of 150.

The unit of accuracy is a unique benchmark problem, never a certificate.
Cross-family overlap is retained when reporting each family, but duplicate or
alternate certificates for the same family/problem do not create extra hits.

Trace was rerun on all 4,187 problems.  The other methods are supplemental:
guarded was rerun on its 2 historical generated hits, completion on its 61, and
sound CNF NF16 on its 17.  Therefore only trace has a fresh full-benchmark
accuracy in this release artifact.

## Primary trace result

`trace_depth_sweep_soundfix_v5` produced 636 certificates and Judge v3 accepted
all 636.  This is 36/130 on order five.  Its ALPS source view covers 624/4,140
equivalence classes, or 625/4,141 original rows after mapping the frozen
duplicate classes.  Of the accepted representatives, 600 use an ALPS-native ID
and 24 use an order-five representative for a class that is also in ALPS.  The
mapped rows are coverage entries, not additional Judge receipts.  Among the
3,551 non-generated rows, 256 reached the configured 2,000 MiB process-tree
limit and 3,295 terminated without a candidate.  There were no infrastructure
errors and no Judge rejection.  The largest certificate is 521,804 bytes.

An earlier 643-problem preflight had 12 misses under a later repeat.  All 12
previous certificates were revalidated and are included in
`results/certificates/trace_fluctuation_archive/`.  Two of those problems were
again solved by the final 636 run, so the archive contributes 10 additional
problems, not 12.  The fresh trace set plus this explicitly non-primary archive
contains 646 unique problems.  These fluctuation certificates are not folded
into the 636 fresh count or the primary four-family union.

## Supplemental families

Guarded regenerated its two historical hits, E22619 and E22634, and both were
accepted.  Neither is covered by any of the other three sound selected family
sets, so guarded remains a small but genuinely complementary method.

Completion uses three separately reported profiles:

1. `completion_hybrid_soundfix_v5` is the best/current main profile.  On the 61
   historical completion hits it generated 60 certificates.  Judge accepted
   57.  E9000534, E9000535, and E9000536 had no terminal Judge response by the
   experiment's 60-minute client waiting cutoff and are recorded as
   `lean_timeout_60m_client_cutoff`, not as rejections.  The remaining problem,
   E9001325, was not generated by this profile.
2. `completion_generalized_index0_full_v1` is a targeted search-profile
   supplement.  It generated and Judge accepted E9001325, adding one problem to
   the main profile's generated set.
3. `completion_indexed_all_v1` is a proof-compilation/search-profile supplement.
   It generated alternative certificates for E9000534--E9000536; all three were
   Judge accepted.  These replace the three policy-cutoff hybrid certificates
   for final coverage and do not count as three additional problems beyond the
   61-problem historical set.

Consequently, the selected completion portfolio has accepted certificates for
all 61 historical completion problems, while the main hybrid profile's own
record remains transparently 60 generated, 57 accepted, 3 client-policy
timeouts, and one no-candidate result.

The repaired `cnf_nf16_reverse_ties_completed_first_sound_v3` regenerated all
17 sound historical NF16 hits and all 17 were accepted.  Fourteen are order-five
problems; its ALPS source view contains five equivalence classes, including two
cross-source representatives.  Older pre-soundness-fix CNF
profiles and NF32/NF64/NF128 experiments are excluded from the final selected
set.

## Judge status and release cleanliness

The public receipt table contains 731 profile/certificate occurrences: 728
accepted and three completion-hybrid client-policy timeouts.  The 728 count
includes cross-profile duplicates and the 12 trace archive certificates; it is
not a solved-problem count.  The selected primary union is 668 unique problems.

There are zero `incorrect`, malformed, or other non-timeout Lean rejections in
the final data.  The three timeout records explicitly state that no terminal
Judge response or rejection was received before the client cutoff.  Their
replacement indexed certificates are independently accepted.

Raw controller logs and raw Judge responses are intentionally excluded because
they contain Sandbox instance IDs, internal control identifiers, private
endpoint fields, and local staging paths.  The published rows use positive
allowlists.  Individual Lean code, its byte length and SHA-256 hash, the public
Judge status, timing, service/proof-policy revisions, and frozen inputs are all
retained for independent checking.
